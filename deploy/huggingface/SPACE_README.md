# IFHAS-NeuroTwin (research demo)

NeuroTwin is a research prototype, not a medical device. It does not diagnose disease or predict a person's health.

- Patients and family members shown here are fictional demonstration accounts with synthetic records.
- Brain Analytics uses separate, de-identified OASIS research scans, shown under the OASIS data-use terms.
  OASIS: Open Access Series of Imaging Studies. Required citations are listed in the source repository
  (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and docs/ASSETS.md).
- The demo database is rebuilt whenever the Space restarts; anything entered here is not kept.
- Model-generated text, where enabled, is a draft for clinician review.

Source code: https://github.com/AKMaasif/neurotwin (MIT licence).
