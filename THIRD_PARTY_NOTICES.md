## Activities

The daily check-in, reaction-time and picture-sequence activities are NeuroTwin's own digital prototypes, marked
"not clinically reviewed" in `backend/app/protocols.py`. They are not copies of licensed instruments (MoCA is not
reproduced), and no published norms or accuracy figures are claimed for them.

The WHO-5 Well-Being Index (1998, English) is included unmodified as a published instrument. Record its copyright
holder's terms and required wording here, and confirm they permit the intended use (including any commercial pilot),
before public release. An Arabic version needs a validated translation, not a machine one.
